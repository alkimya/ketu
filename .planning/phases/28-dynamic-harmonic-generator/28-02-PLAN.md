`calculate_aspects` (scalar), `calculate_aspects_vectorized`, and

surfaces and get dynamic detections that are type-indistinguishable from static

Covers requirements: ASP-06 (dynamic detection scalar/vectorized/batch, per-pair
orbs, i_asp=-2) and ASP-09 (both IndexError guards; off-table resolves via synthetic
